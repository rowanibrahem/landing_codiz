THE DATA ANALYSIS WORKFLOW

From Business Questions to Better Decisions.

رحلة تحليل البيانات مش بتبدأ من Excel أو Power BI؛ بتبدأ من سؤال محتاج إجابة، وبعدها بنمر بمراحل مختلفة لحد ما نوصل لنتائج مفيدة.

  1. 1
    Ask – Understand the Business Question
    • Understand the business problem.
    • Identify stakeholders and their needs.
    • Define useful business questions.
    • Identify the data required.
  2. 2
    Extract – Collect the Data
    • Understand databases and data sources.
    • Use SQL to retrieve data.
    • Work with Excel and CSV files.
    • Understand basic ETL/ELT concepts.
  3. 3
    Clean & Transform – Prepare the Data
    • Handle missing values & identify duplicates.
    • Fix incorrect data types & transform datasets.
    • Validate data quality and consistency.
  4. 4
    Analyze & Model – Discover Insights
    • Explore datasets & perform aggregations.
    • Identify patterns and trends.
    • Build basic data models & apply Python and Pandas.
  5. 5
    Visualize & Communicate – Share Results
    • Create visualizations & interactive Power BI reports.
    • Present findings & communicate insights through data storytelling.
